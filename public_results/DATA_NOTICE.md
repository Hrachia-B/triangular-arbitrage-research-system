# Data notice

The files in `public_results/` contain aggregates and simulation outputs derived
from observations of public exchange market data. Raw exchange streams are not
included.

The result files are provided for research and educational review. They do not
represent real orders, fills, account performance, or trading advice, and they
make no profitability claim. Anyone collecting new observations is responsible
for reviewing and complying with the applicable exchange terms and local
requirements.

Private credentials, authenticated raw responses, logs, and local machine paths
were intentionally excluded from this package.
